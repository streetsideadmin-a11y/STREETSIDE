What Streetside Does

Streetside is a residential bin valet service: we move your trash and recycling bins to the curb before your scheduled collection, and return them to storage afterward. Streetside does not collect, haul, or dispose of garbage. Your existing waste and recycling provider continues to collect and empty your bins as normal — Streetside works alongside them, not in place of them.
