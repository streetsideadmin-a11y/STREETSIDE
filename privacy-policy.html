Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Streetside ("we," "us," "our") operates a residential trash and recycling bin valet service in Southeastern Ohio. This page explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us by filling out a form on this site — the waitlist signup or the "check your address" tool. Depending on which form you use, that may include:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Street address, city, and ZIP code
  • Trash collection day and current waste provider (optional)
  • Number of trash and recycling bins, and where they're normally stored
  • How you heard about Streetside

We do not collect payment information through this site, and there is currently no way to create an account or log in.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Contact you about Streetside service availability in your area
  • Set up and provide service once you become a customer
  • Respond to questions you send us directly
  • Understand where interest in Streetside is coming from, so we know where to open new routes

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes, other than the advertising and analytics tools described under "Cookies & Tracking" below.

Cookies & Tracking

This website uses third-party tools to understand how people find and use it:

  • Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram). Lets us measure whether visitors who arrive from our ads go on to join the waitlist, so we can improve our advertising. It may set cookies in your browser and send Meta information such as your browser and device details, the pages you view, and the fact that you submitted a form. We do not intentionally send the contents of what you type into our forms.
  • Vercel Web Analytics. Gives us overall visit counts and page views, without tracking individual visitors across other websites.

We also record which ad or link brought you here (for example, a campaign tag in the web address) and attach it to your signup so we know which outreach is working. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

You can limit this kind of tracking with your browser's privacy settings, an ad blocker, or Meta's ad preferences. If we add or change these tools, we will update this policy.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Form submissions are stored in a database used to operate this website and are accessible only to Streetside. We take reasonable steps to keep this information secure, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you've submitted at any time by contacting us using the details below. If you no longer wish to be contacted, let us know and we'll remove you from our list.

Children's Privacy

This site and our service are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Streetside grows. Significant changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
