How It Works

Simple. Reliable. Every week.

We handle your bins so you don't have to think about trash day.

Step 01

You Sign Up

Tell us your address, collection day, number of bins, and where your bins are normally stored.

Step 02

We Take Them Out

Before collection, Streetside moves your designated trash and recycling bins to the curb.

Step 03

Your Waste Company Collects

Your existing garbage and recycling provider empties the containers normally.

Step 04

We Bring Them Back

After collection, Streetside returns your emptied bins to their normal storage location.

Done. Trash day handled.

One less chore. Every week.
